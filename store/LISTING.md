## Full description (4000 characters)

```
RecetApp turns what you already have at home into something to cook.

Register the ingredients in your pantry and RecetApp proposes dishes you can
actually make with them, gives you the full recipe, helps you lay out the
week, and tells you what to buy.

WHAT IT CAN DO

- Keep your pantry: what you have, how much, and when it runs low.
- Suggest dishes from that pantry. Choose between only what you already have,
  or dishes that need a couple of extra ingredients.
- Write the full recipe for any dish, with the steps and the amounts, and
  scale it to the number of people eating.
- Plan a week of meals without repeating a dish, and reshuffle a single day
  without losing the rest.
- Build a shopping list from what is running low plus what the week needs,
  and tell you which dish wants each thing.
- Remind you when an ingredient runs low.

HOW IT WORKS

Suggestions are generated with Google Gemini. If you ask for dishes using only
what you have, RecetApp checks the result against your pantry before showing
it - so a dish that quietly needs something you do not own never reaches you.
The same check applies to ingredients you have asked to avoid.

YOUR DATA

Your pantry, your weekly plan and your preferences stay on your device.
Ingredient names are sent to our server and on to Google Gemini only when you
ask for a suggestion or a recipe. There are no accounts and nothing is stored
on our side.
```
